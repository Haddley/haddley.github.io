**Summary.** I printed a new back half for a rear light, in two versions: one that fits the small gap behind a bike mudguard, and one that lets the cable leave through the side. I designed them with Claude Code and measured the original from a photo. Design 2 is the one on the bike.

## What I did
- Printed a copy of the original back half and fixed it in small steps.
- Traced the original from a photo and fitted the part that mates with it.
- Made a separate file for Design 2, with the screws, the gutter and a cable clamp.
- Printed both and fitted Design 2 to the carrier.

## Claims
- The carrier gave two constraints: a bar with slots, and little room behind the light.
  > "There is also not a lot of room between where the light will be mounted and the rear mudguard."
- The carrier bar has no hole for the cable, which is why Design 2 exists.
  > "The bar has no hole in the middle."
- Design 1 saves space by leaving out the flat bracket.
  > "Removing the bracket removed vertical height."
- The carrier slots replace the bracket.
  > "The slots in the carrier do the bracket's job."
- A trap when printing: the file on disk is not necessarily the model.
  > "The STL on disk is not the model"
- Design 2 is the one I used.
  > "Design 2 is the one that I fitted to the bike."

## Outcome
Two printable designs, with STL files to download, and Design 2 mounted on the bike.
