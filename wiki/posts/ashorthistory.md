## Claims
- Microsoft acquired Great Plains in 2000 to enter the small and medium business market.
  > "21, 2000 — Microsoft Corp. and Great Plains Software Inc. today announced plans to join forces in an acquisition designed to provide small and medium companies with the technology necessary to thrive in an increasingly interconnected economy."
- Microsoft acquired Navision in 2002 to enhance its business solutions.
  > "COPENHAGEN, Denmark, May 7, 2002 — Microsoft Corp. today announced it has reached an agreement to acquire Navision, a global provider of integrated business software solutions."
- Project Green was an effort involving 40 percent of the Business Solutions programmers.
  > "In September 2003 Satya Nadella (then Microsoft Business Solutions division's corporate VP of product development) explained that Project Green was an effort involving about 40% of Business Solutions' 1,700 programmers."

- Dataverse was introduced in 2020 as a low-code data platform.

- Navision is the direct ancestor of the product known as Business Central.
  > "Navision is the ancestor of Business Central."
