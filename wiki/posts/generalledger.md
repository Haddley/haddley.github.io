## What I did
- I created a Chart of Accounts from scratch.
- I recorded a purchase transaction for inventory.
- I recorded a sale transaction for the inventory.
- I compared the resulting asset and liability balances against the income and cost of goods sold.

## Claims
- The General Ledger contains the balances of all accounts in the Chart of Accounts.
  > "The General Ledger contains the balances of all accounts in the Chart of Accounts."
- A purchase requires recording the asset value, the vendor liability, and the sales tax.
  > "When we purchase items we will need to record the value of the items (a new asset), the money we owe to the vendor (a new liability) and the sales tax that we will claim back from the authorities."

## Outcome
